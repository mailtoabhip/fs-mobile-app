# Product Overview

Axle is a logistics and transportation management mobile application developed by Delhivery for suppliers and truck operators. The app enables users to:

- Browse and bid on available transportation loads (Intracity, Intercity, Marketplace)
- Manage contract opportunities (Express, Non-Express, Intracity contracts)
- Track and manage bids (Active, Confirmed, Lost, Contract bids)
- Handle trip lifecycle from bidding to completion
- Manage fleet and truck information
- Process payments, wallet transactions, and fuel cards
- Complete KYC verification (PAN, GST, Aadhaar, Address)
- Track earnings, transactions, and ledger details
- Communicate with shippers through bridge numbers

The app serves as a comprehensive platform connecting truck operators with transportation opportunities across Delhivery's logistics network and external marketplace loads.
